# Project Collection Mobile Snap Indicator — QA Notes

The mobile collection now renders a four-point signal indicator immediately below the swipeable card stage. The state tracks the card closest to the stage centre during horizontal scrolling; the active dot receives a short violet confirmation pulse. The indicator is hidden on desktop, announces the centred card to assistive technology, pauses with the global motion control, and becomes static under reduced-motion preferences. A full-page mobile capture completed successfully after the change.
